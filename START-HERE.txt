Sources are versioned implementation evidence, historical guide summaries, or player advice. A source-based statement is not a gameplay observation. Several Magic Mario RS2 sections are unfinished. Redkiwi's later route is a plan. Full copyrighted guide texts are not copied.

The database uses keyword search, not semantic embeddings. It does not train a model, launch the game, or provide a game controller. Binary assets, maps, and live prices are excluded.
